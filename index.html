How to play chess

Chess is a two-player strategy game. White moves first, then players take turns. The goal is to checkmate the opposing king.

Piece moves: Pawns move forward and capture diagonally; rooks move in straight lines; bishops diagonally; queens in straight lines or diagonally; knights move in an L-shape; kings move one square in any direction.

Check: Your king is under attack. In this version, you may move any piece while in check, but if your move leaves your own king in check, you immediately lose.
